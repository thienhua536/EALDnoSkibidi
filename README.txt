EVIDENCE FINDER - HSC STUDY SITE (English EAL/D Module 1 + Biology Module 5)

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click index.html. No internet is needed except for the fonts (it still works without them).
  Your progress is saved in your browser, on this device only. Use My progress > Back up to move it.

WHERE TO ADD THINGS (you almost never need to touch app.js)
  content-reading.js    reading texts and their questions
  content-unseen.js     unseen texts, written questions, practice paper B
  content-practice.js   technique, linking-word and paragraph practice
  content-more.js       40+ extra techniques (cards, lessons and quiz all come from this)
  content-module1.js    Module 1 ideas, Types of text, and their quizzes
  content-topics.js     essay topic maker and writing forms
  content-creative.js   creative writing drills and the Error bank lists
  content-biology.js    Biology Module 5 flashcards
  content-images.js     pictures for flashcards (instructions at the top)
  content-help.js       NESA key words, simpler wording, synonyms
  content-khmer.js      Khmer translations (please have a Khmer speaker check them)
  style.css             colours and fish theme (first 20 lines)
  app.js                the engine: the map at the top explains it

RULES FOR EDITING
  - Copy one existing line or block, change the words, keep the commas and quotation marks.
  - In quiz questions the FIRST option is the correct one. The site shuffles them.
  - After editing, refresh the page. If the page goes blank, open the browser console (F12)
    and read the red error: it names the file and line with the typo.
