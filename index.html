About the HSC exam (first sat under the new syllabus in 2027)

Paper 1, Texts and human experiences, has 10 minutes of reading time and 1 hour 30 minutes of working time. Section I (20 marks) has 4 to 6 short-answer questions on stimulus and/or unseen texts. Section II (20 marks) is one extended response on your prescribed text.

The exam asks for written answers. Use the quiz to practise spotting techniques and evidence, then use Exam practice to write. Exam practice follows the style of an older school assessment (2017 syllabus), so its marks and sections differ from the new HSC paper. Writing is now examined in Paper 2. Check current details with your teacher or NESA.

“Solitude” (first stanza) is by Ella Wheeler Wilcox, 1883. All other texts and images are original practice material written for this site.
